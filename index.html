01 / Failure-aware inferenceEEG

Pipeline schematic · research evaluation

Reliable healthcare MLDec 2025 – Present

Catching confident errors
in EEG sleep staging.

The challenge
A confident prediction can still be wrong. In healthcare, those errors deserve a closer look.
My approach
I combine two experts and use their disagreement to identify and reroute high-risk EEG samples.
≈64.4%relative reduction in accepted-sample high-confidence errors
  • EEG
  • Transformers
  • Risk-aware routing
  • Failure analysis
Methods & evaluation +
  • Combine a Transformer with a feature-based expert for failure-aware inference.
  • Use label mismatch and distributional divergence as cross-model disagreement signals.
  • Evaluate with 20-fold leave-one-subject-out validation on Sleep-EDF-20; identify 1,923 high-confidence errors (HCEs).
  • Escalate 18.8% of samples and reduce the HCE rate among accepted samples to 1.62%, a relative reduction of approximately 64.4%.

The reduction refers to accepted samples after routing, not an overall accuracy gain across all samples. These are research evaluation results.

System schematic

Efficient NLPApr 2025 – Present

Smarter routing for
suicide risk detection.

A cascaded system for social media analysis: handle explicit cases with a lightweight classifier and reserve more intensive reasoning for ambiguous content.

67.6%of inputs filtered at the BERT stage
  • BERT
  • LLM agents
  • Ensembles
Methods & evaluation +
  • Route inputs using text length and the confidence of a fine-tuned BERT classifier.
  • Compare BERT + LLM agent voting for recall with ML voting over LLM-extracted psychological features for interpretability.
  • Reduce the reported cross-domain performance gap to less than 2%.

67.6% is the fraction filtered at the first stage, not a measured reduction in total latency. Related manuscript in preparation: see publication status.
