LLMs & retrievalAug 2026 – Present

Finding evidence.
Understanding agent failures.

I contribute evaluation design and failure analysis to DSH DocsQA, a documentation-QA benchmark spanning 467 questions and 4,860 pages across four products.

5retrieval baselines analyzed across 2,335 evaluations
  • Agentic RAG
  • Retrieval evaluation
  • Evidence grounding
Repository ↗Merged research plan ↗
Methods & findings +
  • Authored a merged research roadmap separating retrieval, evidence grounding, and agent efficiency; identified useful evidence that sparse citation-derived labels can miss.
  • Analyzed candidate and ranking failures: native-link expansion reduced candidate misses by 26.5% without increasing total top-10 citation recovery; the tested reranker lowered Recall@10 by 9.06 percentage points.
  • In a reported 361-question agent comparison, hybrid retrieval used 69.0% fewer tokens per question and improved weighted answer-aspect coverage by 7.58 percentage points, alongside a higher unsupported-claim rate.

My contribution centers on evaluation design and results analysis. The 361-question agent comparison is a separate reported experiment from the 467-question retrieval analysis. Evaluation and results discussion.

02 / Selective escalationNLP

System schematic · research evaluation

Efficient NLPApr 2025 – Present

Smarter routing for
suicide risk detection.

I developed a two-stage framework that handles high-confidence explicit posts with fine-tuned BERT and escalates ambiguous inputs to LLM voting or a psychological-feature ensemble.

67.6%of 23,200 Reddit test posts handled at the BERT stage
  • BERT
  • LLM voting
  • Selective escalation
Preprint ↗Manuscript status ↗
Methods & evaluation +
  • Route inputs using text length and BERT confidence, keeping second-stage processing to 32.4% of the held-out Reddit test set.
  • The feature-ensemble variant achieved 97.99% F1 versus 97.41% for BERT on that test set.
  • Evaluate transfer to synthetic implicit-risk posts to study difficult cases, recall, F1, and routing coverage.

First-stage coverage is not a measured end-to-end latency reduction. The current manuscript is under review at JMIR AI; the linked preprint is an earlier version.

03 / Uncertainty-aware predictionEEG

Initial framework · dissertation research

Reliable ML & biosignalsJan 2026 – Present

Catching confident errors
in EEG sleep staging.

My dissertation investigates how model disagreement and uncertainty can help identify risky predictions, with an emphasis on subject-level evaluation and selective prediction.

1.62%high-confidence-error rate among accepted samples in an initial evaluation
  • EEG
  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Selective prediction
Methods & evaluation +
  • Designed a two-stage reliability framework using cross-model label mismatch and distributional divergence.
  • Completed an initial 20-fold leave-one-subject-out evaluation on Sleep-EDF-20, identifying 1,923 high-confidence errors and escalating 18.8% of samples.
  • Extending this work toward calibration audits, subject-level risk estimation, and risk-controlled selective prediction under held-out-subject evaluation.

The 1.62% metric concerns high-confidence errors among accepted predictions, not the overall classification error rate. These initial research results do not establish a deployment guarantee.

04 / Controller-aware pruningCPS

Model-selection schematic

Efficient ML systemsSep 2023 – Jul 2025

Smaller perception models.
System-level constraints.

I co-developed controller-aware pruning for YOLOv8 perception models, connecting model compression with downstream closed-loop safety requirements.

up to148.33×speedup in pruning search on distance-estimation tasks
  • YOLOv8
  • Model compression
  • Reachability analysis
ACM TCPS paper ↗
Methods & evaluation +
  • Use reachability analysis and maximum reachable-set diameter to characterize how pruning affects closed-loop behavior.
  • Construct regression-based pruning scaling laws to select the smallest model satisfying the evaluated safety constraints.
  • Accelerate pruning search while meeting the evaluated hardware and safety objectives.

148.33× refers to pruning-search speedup, not the inference speedup of the final network.

ML for sensingSep 2021 – Dec 2022

Recognizing motion
without a camera.

I developed signal-processing and deep-learning pipelines for human motion classification from radio-frequency measurements, studying how preprocessing and model choice affect accuracy.

99.1%accuracy for RNN + PCA in the reported 700-sample study
  • RF sensing
  • Signal processing
  • RNNs
  • PCA
Scientific Reports paper ↗
Methods & evaluation +
  • Apply Butterworth low-pass filtering and artifact removal before neural-network inference.
  • Compare network architectures and PCA configurations on 700 sitting and standing samples, averaging results across 10 random training/test splits.
  • RNN + PCA achieved 99.1% accuracy versus 93.8% for the basic neural-network baseline.

The comparison changes both the architecture and preprocessing; the full improvement cannot be attributed to PCA alone.
