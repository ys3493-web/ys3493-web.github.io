Research

Mitigating High-Confidence Errors in Sleep Stage Classification Using EEG Signals

Dec 2025 – Present

Developed a dual-expert (Transformer & Feature-based) two-stage reliability pipeline for EEG, designed for Algorithm Hardening in clinical systems.

Implemented risk-aware routing based on cross-model disagreement (Type-1 label mismatch and Type-2 distributional divergence) to detect and escalate high-risk cases.

Executed 20-fold LOSO evaluation on Sleep-EDF-20, identifying 1,923 High-Confidence Errors (HCEs).

Escalated only 18.8% of samples while reducing accepted-sample HCE rate to 1.62% (≈ 64.4% reduction).

EEG Transformer Reliability Clinical ML

Two-Stage Voting for Robust and Efficient Suicide Risk Detection on Social Media

Apr 2025 – Present

Designed a two-stage voting architecture with length-confidence routing using fine-tuned BERT, filtering 67.6% of inputs for cost-efficient reasoning.

Stage 2 resolves ambiguous cases via BERT + LLM Agents Voting (max recall) and BERT + ML Voting over LLM-extracted psychological features (interpretability).

Achieved up to 99.7% F1 with cross-domain performance gap reduced to <2%.

Deep Learning for Contactless Human Motion Detection (RF-based)

Sep 2021 – Dec 2022

Applied Butterworth low-pass filter for signal processing refinement on RF-based human motion data.

Benchmarked CNN, RNN, ResNet variants — improved baseline accuracy from 93.8% to 98.6%.

Proved the impact of preprocessing over architecture: 99.1% final accuracy with LSTM + PCA.
